scheduler

one background process round-robins every queued/running extraction and clustering job in blocks of {{ block_size }} items (stories for extraction, distinct tag texts for clustering) at a time, grouped by model, so each job's fixed instruction prefix stays warm in the local model's cache instead of losing it on every call — and so extraction and clustering jobs don't thrash Ollama's VRAM by swapping models back and forth on every call between them. It exits on its own once nothing is queued, and respawns automatically the next time an extraction/clustering job is created or this page (or a job's status) is checked.
